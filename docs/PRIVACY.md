# Privacy policy

**The policy itself is [`store/privacy-policy.md`](../store/privacy-policy.md), and that file is the
only copy.** This page used to hold a second, shorter one — written a day apart, saying the same
thing in different words — and two privacy policies in one repository is one policy too many: they
drift, and the one the owner links from Play Console is then not the one a reader of the source
finds.

`store/privacy-policy.md` is the copy kept because it is the complete one: it is **bilingual**, which
an app that is bilingual from its first screen has to be, and it covers the ground Play's form asks
about — what is kept on the device, what the archives necessarily see, retention, children,
changes.

What it says has not changed and is short enough to repeat here: **Protracktor collects nothing.**
No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no account, no identifier read or generated. The
app contacts an archive when you ask it for something from that archive, and nothing else leaves the
device.

`docs/PLAY_STORE.md` has what still has to happen before publishing: the policy must be reachable at
a public URL, and a file in a **private** repository is not one.
