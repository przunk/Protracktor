Seek in C64 (SID) and Atari ST (SNDH) tunes; a spinner shows while it gets there.

A tune with no stated length gets a seek bar up to where it will stop, marked ~.

Opening a Modland author's folder downloads its tunes in the background, three at a time: Wi-Fi only by default (Settings → Online catalogues).

Links to the web player at przunk.github.io open in the app.

Protracktor no longer offers itself as a browser. More buttons have icons, and a file that will not play says more about why.
