From the first round of testers.

One press now fetches the archives: pick what you want, see what each costs, and stop it half way if you change your mind. An empty playlist offers that instead of sending you to a Browse screen with nothing in it.

Every message the app shows is in your language now, not only the screens around it. Buttons are tall enough for accented letters and for larger system text. Uninstalling removes the app's data, as it should.
