The index now keeps everything an archive lists, and notes beside each track whether this build can play it. Adding a decoder later costs a moment of local work instead of downloading the index again.

The first launch after updating rearranges what is already downloaded, so it may take a few seconds. Nothing is lost and nothing is fetched.

Indexing reports both numbers: what the archive holds, and what plays here. Console tunes no longer claim a length the file never stated.
