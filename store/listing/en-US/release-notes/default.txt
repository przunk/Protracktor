The index now keeps everything an archive lists, and notes beside each track whether this build can play it. Adding a decoder later costs a moment of local work instead of downloading the whole index again.

The first launch after updating rearranges what is already downloaded, so it may take a few seconds. Nothing is lost and nothing is fetched.

Indexing now reports both numbers: how much the archive holds, and how much of it plays here. Large numbers read as numbers everywhere.
